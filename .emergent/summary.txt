The user has requested a new, complex, and fully editable rule system for dynamic trading.

- **Price Calculation:**

    2.  Apply modifiers for item blessings/rewards (+15% to +50%).
    3.  Apply cumulative environmental modifiers for Region, Settlement Type, and Historical Context (e.g., war, famine).
    4.  Adjust based on the player's relationship with the NPC (Hostile to Brotherhood) to determine the final  (fair price).
- **NPC Reaction System:**
    1.  Evaluate the percentage difference between the player's offer and the .
    2.  Use a probabilistic system (random d100 roll modified by relationship and context) to determine the outcome.

    4.  Different outcome tables for Buying vs. Selling.
- **NPC Profiles:** The system should eventually support different NPC trader profiles (Greedy, Honorable, Desperate) that alter negotiation behavior.
